Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-19

This policy explains what Pixel Press ("the app"), made by Suki App Studio ("we", "us"), does with information. In short: your pictures and PDFs are processed only on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. The only data that leaves your phone is what the Google advertising service collects to show the banner ads.

1. Your pictures and PDFs

The app only opens the pictures and PDFs that you choose in the system photo picker or file picker, or that you share to the app from another app.

Compressing, resizing, converting, cropping, creating PDFs, saving PDF pages as images and reading or changing photo information (EXIF: date, camera, location and other tags) all happen on your phone. Your files are not uploaded, and we cannot see them.

Results are saved as new files in Pictures/Pixel Press, Download/Pixel Press or a folder you choose. Your originals are changed only when you turn on "Replace original" in Photo info.

"Remove location & share" makes temporary copies without location inside the app's private storage and hands them to the app you choose. These copies are deleted automatically after a day.

2. What is stored on your phone

The app remembers your last options for each tool, the save folder you chose and your file name setting. They stay on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your photo library, camera, location or contacts. It uses the system pickers, which give it access only to the files you choose.

On Android 8 and 9 the app asks for storage permission, only to save results into the Pictures and Download folders.

The app uses internet access only for ads.

4. Advertising

The app shows banner ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and diagnostic data. Google may use cookies or similar technologies on your device for this.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Sharing and sale

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Apart from the advertising described above, no data leaves your phone through the app.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea).

7. Your rights

We do not hold personal information about you. For data processed by Google for ads, you can use the settings above and Google's own tools. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply, and you can ask us to delete it. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version in the app and on our website with a new effective date.
