1. What the App is

The App helps groups make random choices in a fun way: picking one or several people, splitting people into teams, putting them in order, handing out results such as prizes or chores, or picking an option such as a lunch menu, through games such as a prize wheel, a ladder lottery, a marble race, a lane race, card flips, a name reel, a capsule toy machine and a spotlight. It also offers pass-the-phone party games (finger pick, reaction duel, stop at 10, hot potato, pirate barrel, spin the bottle and number bomb), a Secret Santa draw, a penalty card draw and quick tools (coin flip, dice and random numbers), and keeps your lists, groups and past results on your device.

5. Play safely and kindly

Pass-the-phone games involve handing the phone to other people. Hold it firmly, do not throw it, and keep the game away from water and traffic. The penalty cards in the App are light suggestions, and anyone may say no to one. Take care with the penalties, dares or drinks your group attaches to a result: never pressure anyone into something unsafe, illegal, humiliating or against their will, do not drink and drive, and respect people who do not want to take part.
