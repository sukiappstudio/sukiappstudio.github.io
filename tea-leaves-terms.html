1. What the App is

The App is a single-player, interactive tea-leaf reading experience: you write a question, pour a cup of tea with loose leaves in it, drink it, swirl it three times, turn it over onto its saucer and back again, and the reader names the shapes the leaves have left in the cup. It runs entirely on your device. The pattern of the leaves is made by the App's own software from your words and the day, and the readings are fixed texts written for the App. The App keeps your settings and your journal of readings on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
