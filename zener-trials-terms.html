1. What the App is

The App is a parapsychology test game set in an old university laboratory. You can guess Zener cards in runs of twenty-five, whose order is shuffled and sealed (with a cryptographic fingerprint) before you start, and sketch the content of sealed archive photographs and pick them among four. Your results are scored with ordinary statistics (hit counts, exact binomial p-values, z-scores) and summarised as an "ability index". The App keeps your settings and your record on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
