1. What the App is

The App is a single-player ink oracle experience: you hold a question in mind or type it, let drops of ink fall onto water in a basin drawn by the App, move the ink by tilting the phone or with your finger, lay a sheet on it, fold, press and open the sheet, and type up to three words for what you see in the blot. The App then shows signs from its own book of fixed texts, chosen by your words, the blot, the day and the ink, and lines about the blot's shape. It is not a test or assessment of any kind and says nothing about your mind, health or character. The App runs entirely on your device and keeps your settings, folio and codex there. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
