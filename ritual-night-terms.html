The App is a party game in the style of truth or dare, with a horror theme. 5. Play safely

Every player decides for themselves: anyone may refuse any truth or dare, at any time, with no other consequence than the game’s refusal count, and everyone should respect another player’s refusal. Dares are meant to be done in the room where you are playing, in a few seconds or minutes; do not adapt them into anything that could hurt you or anyone else, involve fire, leaving the room at night, strangers or other people’s property, and keep your surroundings in mind when the lights are low. Play only with people who agreed to play. The App has a dark theme, sound and vibration; it contains no flashing images. Turn sound and vibration off in Settings if you prefer. Anyone who is worried or thinking of harming themselves should talk to someone they trust or contact a local helpline.
