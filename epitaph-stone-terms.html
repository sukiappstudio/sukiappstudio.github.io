1. What the App is

The App is a single-player churchyard experience: you choose the shape and material of a gravestone, type a name or nickname, choose a mood and an ornament, and tap the stone to carve an epitaph into it, then keep the stone in a gallery on your device and share it as a picture if you wish. The stones, the churchyard, the fog and the chisel are drawn by the App. The epitaphs are fixed texts written for the App and are chosen by its own software from the name, the day and the mood. They are not about any real person, predict nothing and are not advice. The App runs entirely on your device and keeps your settings and your stones there. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
