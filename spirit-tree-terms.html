1. What the App is

The App is a single-player ritual experience inspired by the Thai folk belief in the spirit of the takian tree: you greet her, hang a garland, light incense and rub powder into her bark until numerals appear, and she speaks to you. It runs entirely on your device. The numerals come from a fixed calculation (the tree's seed and the draw date); they are not predictions, they do not come from any lottery operator, and they do not change anyone's chances. The App keeps your settings and notebook on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
