1. What the App is

The App is a single-player, interactive fortune-cookie experience: a small bakery after closing, one cookie a night in a paper bag, cracked between two thumbs, and a folded slip pulled out from between the halves. It runs entirely on your device. The slips, the lucky numbers and the word for the night are fixed texts written for the App, picked by the night and by a number drawn once on your device; the App keeps your settings, your album of slips and your run of nights on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.

The App is designed as a quiet, atmospheric experience with dim light, sound and vibration. If you find it unsettling, stop using it; sound and vibration can be turned off in Settings.
