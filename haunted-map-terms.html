1. What the App is

The App is an atlas of places that published sources, mainly Wikipedia, describe as reportedly haunted. It shows them on a map, in a list sorted by distance, in search results and in dossiers with a short summary written by us, and it keeps a journal of your visits on your device. A place's inclusion means only that such reports exist in those sources; the App makes no claim that anything paranormal happens at any place. The data may be incomplete, outdated or wrong, and coordinates are approximate. The App has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
