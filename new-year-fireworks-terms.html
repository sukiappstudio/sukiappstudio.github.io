1. What the App is

The App is a New Year countdown and a fireworks toy in one. It counts down to midnight in your time zone or in a city you choose, shows when the new year reaches each listed time zone, and lets you launch fireworks on the screen with your fingers, collect shell types, watch choreographed shows and make a wish card to share. The fireworks are drawn and their sounds are made by the App on your device. The times are calculated from your device's clock and its time-zone database; they are for general interest, and the App is not an official time service. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
