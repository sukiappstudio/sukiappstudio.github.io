1. What the App is

The App is a single-player ritual experience inspired by the limpia con huevo, the egg cleanse practised in Mexico and across Latin America: you choose what you bring, light a candle, roll an egg over a figure from the head down, break it into a glass of water and hear how the signs in the glass are traditionally read. It runs entirely on your device. The signs come from a fixed calculation (the day, your choice and how the egg was rolled); they are not a diagnosis, a prediction or a statement about you. The App keeps your settings and notebook on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
