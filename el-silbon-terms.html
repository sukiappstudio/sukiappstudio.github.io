1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror ritual based on a folk legend of the plains of Venezuela and Colombia. You go out onto the llano at night and hear a ghost's whistle; by an old rule a loud whistle means he is far and a faint one means he is close, and you answer each whistle by running or keeping still until dawn. A night lasts about seven minutes and waits while the App is not on screen. The story, the whistle and everything that happens are fiction created by the App. The App keeps your settings and your past nights on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
