1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror experience that recreates the Bloody Mary mirror ritual from Western folklore. Your phone's front camera is shown on the screen as a candle-lit mirror; you say her name three times, and the reflection slowly changes, with sound and vibration, until the lights come back on. It is a work of fiction and entertainment. It keeps your settings and progress on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
