1. What the App is

The App is a single-player, interactive love-ritual experience: at a candle-lit dressing table you type two first names, choose one of five folk love rituals (peeling an apple, plucking a daisy, watching two flames, knotting a red thread, breathing on a mirror) and perform it with your fingers; a card then gives the night's answer with a verse, a favoured day and hour and an omen. It runs entirely on your device. The answers are produced by the App's own software from fixed texts written for it and depend only on the two names and the night; nothing is measured about you or anyone else. The App keeps the names, your settings and your journal on your device. It has no accounts, no chat, no contact or dating features and no multiplayer features.
