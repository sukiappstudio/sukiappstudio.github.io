Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Tomte: Yule Porridge mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App is a seasonal folk-tale ritual: on the nights of December you cook a bowl of porridge, carry it to a barn step and read in the morning what the house's spirit made of it. Everything in it is fiction made by the App. The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. Your settings, the nights of the current season, past seasons and the pages of the verse book you have read are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Settings (on your device only). Sound, vibration, and whether you want the optional morning note.

The season and the record (on your device only). For each night of a season the App keeps the date, how well the porridge was stirred, whether the butter was put on, what the morning found (the state of the bowl and the small signs, all chosen from the App's own fixed lists), whether you used the optional "second bowl", the house's favour, and whether you have read that morning. For a finished season it keeps how Christmas Eve ended. Up to 6 past seasons are kept. You can clear the whole record in Settings.

The morning note (optional, on your device only). If you turn it on, the App asks Android to wake it at about seven in the morning after a night on which you set a bowl out (an inexact alarm that may arrive some minutes late) and shows one ordinary notification from the App. Nothing is sent anywhere; the alarm is put back after a restart of the phone or a change of the clock. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never during a night's rite or a morning: a banner at the bottom of the home screen, the book and the record, after your first night kept; now and then a full-screen ad when you begin a new night (never in the first app session after install, never the first night of a session, at least two minutes apart, limited per session and per day); and a short video only if you tap the "second bowl" offer after a bad morning, which says what it gives: the morning read again with a buttered bowl. Advertisements are limited to content rated PG or lower. Some advertisements may be served by Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. The SDK collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. It does not receive your record. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings lets you change it later. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. Notifications (Android 13 and later, optional: the morning note), starting after a restart (to put the morning note back on the clock), vibration (the spoon against the pot, the bowl on the stone, the morning's signs; switchable in Settings) and network access (advertising). The App does not use the microphone, the camera, your location, contacts, call log, phone or SMS, photos or files, and it does not use exact alarms or full-screen notifications.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Keeping the season, the record and the book, scheduling the morning note, and running the App: providing the functions you request (you set every bowl out yourself and choose the note yourself), and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Your record is not transmitted to anyone. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your settings, the season, the record and the book stay on your device until you clear them in the App, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The App uses Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on on your device, this data may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted, and may be moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: clear the record in the App's settings, or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn the morning note, sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings; notifications can also be turned off in Android Settings at any time.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway: the GDPR / UK GDPR rights in section 6 apply; you may complain to your local supervisory authority (in Sweden IMY, in Norway Datatilsynet, in Denmark Datatilsynet, in Finland the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman). Data received by Google in the United States is described in section 4.

Republic of Korea: items processed, purposes and retention periods are those in sections 2, 3 and 5. Personal information is provided or transferred overseas only to Google LLC (United States and other countries where it operates): the advertising data in section 2, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; time and method: transmitted over the network while an advertisement is shown; retention: according to Google's policy. You may refuse this transfer by declining ad consent or turning off ad personalisation; personalised advertising will then not be shown. Your record is not provided or transferred to anyone. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. Automatically collected information: the App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. Personal information is destroyed by deleting it in the App or deleting the App's data, and e-mails by deleting them from our mailbox. Person responsible for personal information protection: the representative of Suki App Studio, sukiappstudio@gmail.com. You may also seek help from the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972). United States (including California): we do not sell personal information for money. You may opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising through the choices in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. International transfers take place as described in section 4.

Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information; advertising data received by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries, as described in section 4.

India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to nominate and the right to grievance redressal through the contact below.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
