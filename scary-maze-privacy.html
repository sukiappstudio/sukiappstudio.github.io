Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Steady Hand: The Scary Maze mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. Your settings, the scare set you chose, and the reaction cards the App makes are stored only on your device, in the App's own private folder, and are never uploaded or sent to us. The front camera is used only after the person holding the phone has pressed the consent button on their own screen, and the frames it keeps never leave the device unless someone shares the card. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

The reaction camera (optional, by agreement only). The App can keep six still frames from the front camera over the three seconds in which the figure appears, and print them on the reaction card. This happens only if the person holding the phone presses "Yes, keep my reaction" on the hand-over screen for that round and grants the camera permission; the agreement is for one round only and is never remembered. Until then the camera is not opened at all. Once agreed, the camera runs from the moment the round begins until the card is made; every frame is discarded unread except the six kept at the end, which are held in memory, drawn onto the card, and then released. No video file is recorded, no frame is analysed for faces or identity, and nothing is sent anywhere. If the permission is refused, or the device has no camera, the round runs without a camera and the card carries the numbers only.

Reaction cards (on your device only). For each round the App stores one card image (1080 x 1350 pixels: the figure, the six frames if they were kept, how far the finger got, how long it took, how many restarts) and a small record (the date and time, the scare set, the corridor reached, the time, the restarts, whether frames were kept). Cards are kept in the App's private folder and are excluded from Android backups. You can tear up any card in the record; that removes it and its frames from the phone.

Sharing (only when you choose). A card leaves your device only if you tap Share: the App hands the card file to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. We do not receive it. Because a card may show another person's face, share it only with that person's agreement (see the Terms of Service).

Settings and counters (on your device only). Sound, vibration, language, the scare set last chosen, whether the camera is offered, whether the hidden sets have been opened, whether the first-launch notice has been read, the number of rounds handed over, and whether the camera permission was refused.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never over the hand-over screen, a corridor or the figure: a banner at the bottom of the home screen and the record; now and then a full-screen ad when a new round is handed over (never in your first session, never before the first round of a launch, at least two minutes apart, limited per day); and a short video only if you choose to watch one, to open the hidden sets. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. The App asks for the camera (optional, only after the consent button, see above), network access for advertising, and vibration (the start pad, a wall, a corridor cleared, the figure; switchable in Settings). It does not use your location, microphone, contacts or files, and it does not read your photo gallery.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Running the round, making and keeping your cards and settings: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app. Keeping reaction frames: the consent of the person holding the phone, given on the hand-over screen for that round.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your cards, their frames and your settings stay on your device until you tear up a card, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Cards are excluded from Android Auto Backup; the small settings file may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted. Camera frames other than the six on a card are discarded within the round. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: tear up any card in the record (this also removes its frames), or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings. Turn the reaction camera off for every round in the set-up ("Offer the reaction camera"). Withdraw the camera permission at any time in Android's app settings; the App then runs every round without a camera.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. If you appear on a card kept on someone else's phone, ask that person to tear it up. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland: the GDPR / UK GDPR rights in section 6 apply; you may complain to your local supervisory authority. Data received by Google in the United States is described in section 4.

Republic of Korea: items processed, purposes and retention periods are those in sections 2, 3 and 5. Personal information is provided or transferred overseas only to Google LLC (United States and other countries where it operates): the advertising data in section 2, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; time and method: transmitted over the network while an advertisement is shown; retention: according to Google's policy. You may refuse this transfer by declining ad consent or turning off ad personalisation; personalised advertising will then not be shown. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. Automatically collected information: the App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. Personal information is destroyed by deleting the App's data, and e-mails by deleting them from our mailbox. Person responsible for personal information protection: the representative of Suki App Studio, sukiappstudio@gmail.com. You may also seek help from the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972). Children under 14 may not use the App without the consent of their legal guardian.

United States (including California): we do not sell personal information for money. You may opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising through the choices in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. International transfers take place as described in section 4.

Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information; advertising data received by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries, as described in section 4.

India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to nominate and the right to grievance redressal through the contact below.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App, or appears on a card, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its cards and settings in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, keeps camera frames in memory only until the card is drawn, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
