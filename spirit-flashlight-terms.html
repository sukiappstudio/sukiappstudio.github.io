1. What the App is

The App is a single-player flashlight session experience: an old torch lies in a rendered room, you loosen its head, ask questions that can be answered yes or no, watch whether the beam comes on, then thank it and tighten the head to close the session. It runs entirely on your device. The torch's answers are chosen by the App's own software from your words, the session and the day; the same question in the same session always gets the same answer. Nothing in the App communicates with any person, spirit or other party, measures anything about them, or shows a message from anyone; the sensor readings it shows are your phone's ordinary readings. The App keeps your settings and your log on your device. It has no accounts and no features for communicating with other users.
