1. What the App is

The App is an interactive folk-tale ritual drawn from the Nordic tradition of leaving porridge for the farm's house spirit at Christmas. On the nights of December (or, outside the season, in practice nights that follow one another) you stir a pot of porridge with your finger, put the butter on or leave it off, carry the bowl to a barn step and go to bed; in the morning the App shows what became of the bowl and the small signs around it, counts the house's favour, and reads two lines of a verse; on Christmas Eve the season ends in one of several ways. All of it is fiction created by the App: the bowl, the signs, the favour and the figure follow from your own choices and the date, and nothing in the App observes your home or anyone in it. The App keeps your settings and your record on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
