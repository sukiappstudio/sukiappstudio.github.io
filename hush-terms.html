1. What the App is

The App is a single-player horror experience: seven nights in an invented house, each with its own listener, which you survive by staying quiet until dawn. The App measures how loud it is around you with the microphone (if you allow it) or follows your thumb on the screen, and reacts with pictures, synthesised sounds and vibration. It keeps a record of your nights on your device. The house, the listeners, their stories, pictures and sounds are made for the App; they are fiction and are not about any real person, place or event. The App has no accounts, no chat and no online features.

5. Play safely

The App is designed to frighten. With the full scare it can end a night with a sudden picture and a loud sound; choose the gentle ending in Settings if you prefer, and keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially with headphones. The App does not use flashing or strobing light. Play seated, somewhere safe, and not while walking, driving or doing anything that needs your attention. If you have a heart condition, anxiety or another condition that sudden scares may affect, use the gentle ending or do not use the App. Take breaks and stop whenever you want. Anyone who is worried or thinking of harming themselves should talk to someone they trust or contact a local helpline.
