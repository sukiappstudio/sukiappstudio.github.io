1. What the App is

The App is a single-player moon calendar and ritual experience. It calculates the moon's phases, rise and set times, eclipses and the traditional names of the full moons for a city you choose, and lets you write an intention at the new moon, seal it, open it at the full moon, write what you let go of and keep a page for each full moon. Its texts are fixed texts of its own, chosen by the date and by what you wrote. The figures are calculated for general interest and are not intended for navigation or any purpose that needs certified accuracy. The App does not foretell anything, and its rites do not cause anything to happen. It runs entirely on your device and keeps your settings and journal there. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
