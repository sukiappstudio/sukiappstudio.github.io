1. What the App is

The App is an investigation journal experience: you open a case, type where you are and who came along, tick the signs you noticed from a list of fourteen, let a deduction table narrow them to one of twelve names drawn from folklore, put up to three questions to the site, and file the result as a typed case card you can share. The App measures nothing and reaches no one: the signs are what you tick, the classification is a score of your ticks against a fixed table, and every answer is a line chosen by the App's own software from the case number, your words and the classification. Nothing in the App communicates with any person, spirit or other party, or shows a message from anyone. The App keeps your settings and your cases on your device. It has no accounts and no features for communicating with other users. It is not connected with, endorsed by or related to any video game, and the names of the twelve entities are taken from folklore.
