1. What the App is

The App is a single-player wishing ritual: you write a wish, choose a coin, flick it into a well drawn by the App, and read a short answer from the well, which is kept in a wish jar on your device. The well, the coins, the water and the sounds are made by the App's own software; the answer is chosen from fixed texts written for the App, based on the words of your wish and the date. The App runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
