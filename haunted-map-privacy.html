Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Haunted Map: Paranormal Spots mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. Its atlas of places reported as haunted, derived from Wikipedia and Wikidata, is bundled inside the App and searched on your device. Your settings and your journal of visits are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. If you allow it, the App uses your approximate location only on your device, to sort places by distance; it is never stored or sent anywhere. The App connects to the internet to load map tiles from OpenStreetMap, to show advertising provided by Google AdMob and to fetch the list of our other apps, as described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Settings (on your device only). Distance units, language, ambience sound and haptics.

The journal (on your device only). For each visit you log, the App keeps the place (its identifier and name), the date and time, and the note you write (up to 500 characters). You can edit or delete entries and clear the whole journal in the App. The App also keeps which hidden sites you have revealed and until when, and the number of place dossiers you have opened, which is used to space out advertisements.

Approximate location (optional, on your device only). Only when you tap "Light the compass" on the Nearby screen does the App ask for the approximate location permission, with its reason shown on the screen. If you grant it, Android gives the App an approximate position, which the App uses in memory on your device to sort places by distance, to show the distance and bearing in a place's dossier and to centre the map. Your location is never stored, is never sent by the App to us or to anyone else, and is not used by the App for advertising. The App does not request precise location. If you refuse, you choose a city or region by name instead; that lookup runs inside the bundled data on your device. You can change the permission at any time in Android Settings. While a dossier is open the App may read the phone's orientation sensors (the compass) in memory to turn the bearing arrow; these readings are not stored.

Map tiles (OpenStreetMap). The map is drawn from map tiles loaded over the internet from the tile servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (tile.openstreetmap.org). To deliver them, those servers receive your device's IP address, the App's identifier (as its user agent) and which tiles are requested, that is, the area and zoom level you look at on the map. Loaded tiles are kept in the App's cache folder on your device. The OpenStreetMap Foundation processes this data as described at https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. We receive none of it.

Source links. Each place links the Wikipedia page it is derived from. If you tap the link, the page opens in your browser and the Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy applies (https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).

Share card. If you tap "Share card", the App draws an image of one place dossier, saves it in its own cache folder and hands it to the app you choose through the Android share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you tap, and we never receive the image.

Our other apps. The "More from the studio" list is downloaded from our website (sukiappstudio.github.io, hosted by GitHub) at most once a day. GitHub receives your IP address to deliver it; nothing about you is sent.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never over the map: a banner at the bottom of the Nearby, Search and Journal lists; now and then a full-screen ad when you open a place dossier (never the first dossier after you open the App, at least two minutes apart, limited to a few per day); and a short video only if you choose "Reveal hidden sites nearby", which unlocks ten extra places near you for 24 hours. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. Network access (map tiles, advertising and the list of our apps), approximate location (optional, as described above) and vibration (haptics; switchable in Settings). The App does not use precise location, the camera, the microphone, contacts, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Showing the atlas, the map and the distances, and keeping your journal and settings: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

The OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom) receives the map tile requests described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, while the map is shown. GitHub, Inc. (United States) receives your IP address when the App downloads the list of our apps. The location from the location permission is never passed to any of them.

5. Retention

Your journal and settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App; map tiles stay in the App's cache until Android or you clear it. Your location is not kept at all. The App uses Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on on your device, your journal and settings may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted, and may be moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: delete journal entries or the whole journal in the App, or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn location access off at any time in Android Settings, and sound and haptics in the App's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland: the GDPR / UK GDPR rights in section 6 apply; you may complain to your local supervisory authority. Data received by Google in the United States is described in section 4.

Republic of Korea: items processed, purposes and retention periods are those in sections 2, 3 and 5. Location information: the App uses the device's approximate location only on the device, in memory, to calculate distances; Suki App Studio does not collect, store or transmit location information, and it is not provided to any third party. Personal information is provided or transferred overseas to: (1) Google LLC (United States and other countries where it operates): the advertising data in section 2, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; time and method: transmitted over the network while an advertisement is shown; retention: according to Google's policy; (2) the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom): the IP address, the App's identifier and the requested map tiles, to deliver the map; time and method: transmitted over the network while the map is shown; retention: according to its policy. You may refuse the transfer to Google by declining ad consent or turning off ad personalisation; personalised advertising will then not be shown. The map cannot be loaded without the transfer to the OpenStreetMap Foundation, but the atlas, search and dossiers still work offline. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. Automatically collected information: the App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. Personal information is destroyed by deleting the App's data, and e-mails by deleting them from our mailbox. Person responsible for personal information protection: the representative of Suki App Studio, sukiappstudio@gmail.com. You may also seek help from the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972). United States (including California): we do not sell personal information for money. You may opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising through the choices in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. International transfers take place as described in section 4.

Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information; advertising data received by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries, as described in section 4.

India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to nominate and the right to grievance redressal through the contact below.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
