Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Ghost Lens: SLS Camera mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. The camera picture is read on your device, in memory, to drive the viewfinder, the filters and the tracker; it is never recorded unless you press the shutter, and nothing from the camera is ever uploaded. The frames you capture are saved only in the App's own private folder on your device, without location data, and leave the phone only if you share one yourself. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Camera (on your device only). With your permission, the App shows the back camera's picture in the viewfinder and reads a low-resolution copy of each frame (about 240 by 320 pixels) in memory to compute brightness and edge maps for the tracker and to paint the night and thermal filters. The frames are discarded as soon as the next one arrives. No frame is stored, analysed for faces, or sent anywhere. The camera is used only while the viewfinder is open and stops when you leave it or power the lens down. Refusing the permission leaves the lens cap on; nothing else in the App needs the camera.

Captures (on your device only). When you press the shutter, the App composes a JPEG of the current picture with the overlays of the viewfinder and a timestamp, and saves it in its private folder (files/captures). The capture carries no location data and no identifier of you. You can view, share and delete captures in the App's gallery; deleting removes the file from the device. Captures are excluded from Android backup and from device-to-device transfer. The number of captures is kept for the home screen readout.

Flashlight. The "light" key switches the camera's torch on and off while the viewfinder is open. Nothing is recorded.

Settings and counters (on your device only). Audio, haptics, sensitivity and language; the number of times the lens was powered on, which also spaces out advertisements; and, after you watch a rewarded video, the time until which the sensor gain is boosted.

Session seed. Each launch draws a random session number that seeds the tracker and is printed on captures. It is not derived from you or your device and is not stored.

Touches. The App reads your taps on the screen only to operate the rig. They are used in memory and never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never on, under or over the viewfinder: a banner at the bottom of the home and gallery screens, after the lens has been used once; now and then a full-screen ad when you power the lens on, before the viewfinder opens (never the first time you open the App after installing it, never the first power-on of a session, at most every second power-on and two minutes apart, limited to a few per day); and a short video only if you choose to watch one to boost the sensor gain for five minutes. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. Camera (the viewfinder, see above), network access (advertising only) and vibration (a tick when a form fades, a tap on the shutter; switchable in Settings). The App does not use your location, microphone, contacts, photos, files or any other storage, and does not read your existing photos.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Showing the viewfinder, saving your captures and settings and running the rig: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your progress and settings stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The App uses Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on on your device, this data may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted, and may be moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
