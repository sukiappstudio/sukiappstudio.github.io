1. What the App is

The App is a single-player wishing ritual: you write a wish, blow on a dandelion clock drawn by the App (with your breath through the microphone, or with a finger), watch the seeds fly and become stars, and read a short answer from the meadow, which is kept in a wish sky on your device. The meadow, the dandelion, the seeds and the sounds are made by the App's own software; the answer is chosen from fixed texts written for the App, based on the words of your wish, the date and how many seeds flew. The App runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
