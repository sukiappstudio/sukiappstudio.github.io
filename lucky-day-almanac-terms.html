1. What the App is

The App is a traditional almanac reference: a month calendar in the manner of the old printed almanacs of Japan and Korea that shows, for every day from 1900 to 2100, the lunisolar date, the six-day cycle (rokuyō), the twelve day officers and the folk marks of those traditions (ichiryū-manbai-bi, tensha-nichi, fujōju-bi, son-eomneun-nal), with short explanations, fixed "good for" ratings for six everyday purposes, a finder for favourable days, an exportable year sheet and optional notices for days you mark. Everything is computed on your device from the calendar's own rules; the same day always gives the same reading. The App does not use artificial intelligence, does not use your date of birth or any personal detail, and does not predict events. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
