1. What the App is

The App is an atmospheric overnight recorder. When you start a night watch, it listens to your room through the phone's microphone and keeps short recordings of the moments that rise above the room's quiet, then shows them in a morning report where you can play them normally, slowed, reversed or filtered. Over the recordings the App lays an interpretive layer, the names it gives each moment and the words its "decoder" shows and speaks, which is generated by the App's own software from the recording's measurements; it is entertainment and makes no claim about the cause of any sound. The App is not a medical device: it does not measure, diagnose or improve sleep, snoring, breathing or any health condition, and it is not a security, baby or alarm monitor. The App keeps your recordings, reports and settings on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
