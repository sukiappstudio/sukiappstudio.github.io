1. What the App is

The App tells original horror stories in the form of text-message conversations: messages arrive with pauses as if someone were typing, and at some points you pick a reply that changes how the story goes. One new story becomes available each night at midnight, local time. The conversations, their senders, names, numbers and places are written by us in advance and bundled with the App; the App does not send or receive real messages and cannot read your text messages or other chats. It has no accounts and no multiplayer features.
