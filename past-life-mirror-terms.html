1. What the App is

The App is a single-player, interactive experience inspired by past-life regression: before an antique mirror you enter a name and a date of birth, a candle is lit, you breathe with its flame while a voice counts you down, your reflection appears in the glass, and a past life is told. It runs entirely on your device. The lives, the voice and the scenes are produced by the App's own software from fixed texts and public-domain paintings; the same name and date always give the same lives. The App keeps your settings and your list of remembered lives on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.

5. Use safely

The App is designed as an atmospheric experience with candlelight, a slow breathing guide, a spoken countdown, sound and vibration. The breathing and the countdown are an atmospheric effect, not hypnosis therapy or any treatment. Use it sitting down, in a safe place. If you have epilepsy or a condition affected by flickering light, slow breathing or deep relaxation, or if you feel dizzy, unwell or distressed, stop at once; sound, the voice and vibration can be turned off in Settings. Do not use the App while driving, walking in traffic or doing anything that needs your attention.
