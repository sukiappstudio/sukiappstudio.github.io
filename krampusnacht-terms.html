1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror fiction drawn from the Alpine folk figure of the Krampusnacht. Over five nights you keep a list at a window: you light a candle, tick good deeds from the App's own fixed list, choose a confession and strike marks from a name; on the fifth night, at an hour you choose, the App lets you know with an ordinary notification and a fictional call plays inside the App: the character speaks, you choose your answers, and the year reaches one of several endings. All of it is fiction. The call is not a real phone call: the App does not use the telephone network, does not call or text anyone, and does not show the system's call screen. The character, the lines and the voice (made by your phone's text-to-speech engine) are created by the App. The App keeps your settings, the optional name on the list and your record on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features. The App is meant for adults; it is not a tool for frightening or disciplining children, and you should not write a child's name on the list.
