Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Bloody Mary mobile app (the "App"). The App recreates the Bloody Mary mirror ritual: your phone's front camera becomes a candle-lit mirror, you say her name three times, and the reflection changes. This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. The camera picture is shown on your screen only and is never saved, never analysed to identify you, and never leaves your device. The microphone is used only to notice that you have spoken; nothing is recorded. Your settings and how many rituals you have performed are stored only on your device. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which collects the data described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Camera (on your device only, never stored). While a ritual is running, the App draws the live picture from your front camera on the screen, with effects, as the mirror. The frames go straight from the camera to the graphics processor of your phone and are discarded as each new frame arrives. One frame is kept in graphics memory during the ritual so the reflection can "lean out of the glass" at the end; it is discarded when the ritual ends or the App goes to the background. No picture is saved to storage, no face recognition or face detection is performed, and no picture is ever sent to us or to anyone else. The camera is active only on the ritual screen. Permission is requested the first time you begin a ritual; without it the ritual cannot start.

Microphone (on your device only, never recorded). During the part of the ritual where you say her name, the App measures the loudness of the sound in the voice frequency range about fifty times per second to notice that a short phrase was spoken. The sound itself is used in memory for that one number and discarded at once; no words are recognised, no recording is made and no audio ever leaves your device. The microphone is active only on that screen. Permission is optional: without it you tap the mirror each time you say her name.

Ritual progress and settings (on your device only). The App keeps in its private storage: the number of rituals you have performed, which names you have unlocked, the name you last chose, sound, vibration and language choices, and the count of finished rituals, which is also used to space out advertisements.

Touches. The App reads your taps on the screen only to run the ritual. They are used in memory and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never during a ritual: a banner at the bottom of the home and result screens, after your first finished ritual; now and then a full-screen ad when you start another ritual (never on the way home), limited to a few per day; and a short video only if you choose to watch one to unlock a deeper name. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

"More from the studio". On request, the App downloads a short list of our own apps and their icons from our website so it can show them. That request contains no personal information beyond what any web request carries (your IP address, received by GitHub Pages, which hosts the site).

Permissions. Besides camera, microphone and network access as described above, the App asks for no permission except vibration (used for the ritual's effects and switchable in Settings). It does not use your location, contacts, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Showing the mirror, hearing the three calls, keeping your progress and settings: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app. Camera and microphone are used only after you grant the Android permission, which you can withdraw at any time.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6. Camera pictures and microphone sound are never transferred to anyone.

5. Retention

Camera frames and microphone sound are not retained: they exist in memory for the moment they are shown or measured, and the one frame kept for the end of the ritual is discarded when the ritual ends. Your progress and settings stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The App uses Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on on your device, your settings and progress (never any picture or sound) may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted, and may be moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Camera and microphone: withdraw either permission at any time in Android Settings (Apps > Bloody Mary > Permissions). The ritual needs the camera; it works without the microphone.

Your data on the device: delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland: the GDPR / UK GDPR rights in section 6 apply; you may complain to your local supervisory authority. Data received by Google in the United States is described in section 4.

Republic of Korea: items processed, purposes and retention periods are those in sections 2, 3 and 5. Personal information is provided or transferred overseas only to Google LLC (United States and other countries where it operates): the advertising data in section 2, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; time and method: transmitted over the network while an advertisement is shown; retention: according to Google's policy. You may refuse this transfer by declining ad consent or turning off ad personalisation; personalised advertising will then not be shown. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. Automatically collected information: the App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. Camera and microphone data are processed only on the device and never stored. Personal information is destroyed by deleting the App's data, and e-mails by deleting them from our mailbox. Person responsible for personal information protection: the representative of Suki App Studio, sukiappstudio@gmail.com. United States (including California): we do not sell personal information for money. You may opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising through the choices in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. International transfers take place as described in section 4.

Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information; advertising data received by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries, as described in section 4.

India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to nominate and the right to grievance redressal through the contact below.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is a horror experience and is not directed to children. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, keeps camera and microphone data in memory only, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
