1. What the App is

The App is a planning aid for anglers. For a spot you choose, it shows tide predictions, weather, wave and sea-temperature forecasts, sun and moon times, a fishing index with the reasons behind it, a go / no-go safety check, the species in season and a map. It also offers a catch log, reminders for the tide and the best fishing time, a home-screen widget and tools such as a knot guide, a trip checklist, a length-to-weight estimate and links to official fishing rules.

4. An aid, not a guarantee

Tide, weather, wave, sea-temperature, sun and moon information in the App consists of predictions and estimates. It can be wrong, incomplete or late. Tide predictions are calculated from harmonic constants for a nearby station and do not include the effects of wind, air pressure, storm surges, waves or river flow, so actual water levels and times of high and low water can differ, especially far from the station. Forecasts come from third-party models with limited resolution, are refreshed only while the App is open and online, and may be out of date or unavailable. Wave forecasts describe the sea offshore, not the conditions at a particular beach, rock or harbour.

The fishing index, the best times and the go / no-go safety check are automatic assessments of these predictions against general limits. A "go" does not mean that conditions are safe for you, your experience, your boat or kayak, or your spot. The App cannot see dangers such as rogue waves, slippery rocks, strong currents, lightning or a rising tide cutting off your way back.

The App is not for navigation and never replaces official weather and marine warnings, instructions from coast guards, harbour masters and other authorities, local knowledge or your own judgement. Check official sources and the conditions on site before and during every trip, wear a life jacket on rocks and on the water, and do not go out if in doubt. You are responsible for your own safety and for that of the people with you.
