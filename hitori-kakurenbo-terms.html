1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror experience built around a well-known urban legend, hitori kakurenbo (one-man hide and seek). It guides you through the steps of the legend on your phone's real clock, shows the doll on the screen as you prepare it, put it in the water, stab it and burn it, and plays sounds, vibrations and visual effects that tell a story of fear while you hide. The App does not summon anything, cannot predict or influence events, and makes no claim about the supernatural. It keeps your names, the doll, your journal and your settings on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.

You must be at least 13 years old to use the App. If you are below the age at which you can consent to the processing of your personal data where you live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries), you may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. Do not use the App if you are prone to anxiety attacks, have a heart condition that frightening content could affect, or are sensitive to flashing or sudden images and sounds.

3. Your safety comes first

The legend involves a stuffed doll, rice, nail clippings, a needle and thread, a blade, salt water, a bathtub full of water, a dark house and, at the end, fire. You decide whether to use any real object at all; the App works entirely with the doll on the screen. If you do use real objects, you do so at your own risk and must act responsibly: handle blades and needles with care and put them away; never swallow salt water and spit it out if you feel unwell; never leave a filled bathtub where a child or an animal could reach it; do not walk through a dark house in a way that could make you fall; and if you burn anything, do it outdoors on a surface that does not burn, away from anything flammable, with water at hand, and never indoors. Do not perform the steps while driving, in traffic or anywhere that needs your attention. Stop at once if you feel unwell. Nothing in the App requires you to put yourself or others in any danger, and we are not responsible for injury or damage that results from how you choose to act around real blades, water, fire or darkness.

4. The real clock and leaving the App

The night runs on your phone's clock: the ritual can be begun from two o'clock in the morning, the words are said at three, and the hiding must end within two hours, whether or not the App is open. Keeping the screen on for an hour or more uses battery; the App does not lock the device and you can leave it at any time. We make no promise that a night in progress survives a restart, a battery that runs out, a change of the system time or an update.
