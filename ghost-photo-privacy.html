Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Ghost Photo: Haunted Frame mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. The photographs you take or pick, the prints the App develops from them, your album and your settings are stored only on your device, in the App's own private folder, and are never uploaded or sent to us. The camera's frames are shown on the screen and the one you photograph is written to that folder; nothing is written to your phone's gallery. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Camera (optional). With your permission, the App shows the camera's live picture on the screen as the viewfinder. The frames are not stored, analysed for faces or people, or sent anywhere. When you press the shutter, the frame on the screen at that moment is reduced to about 1,400 pixels and saved as a photograph in the App's private folder (files/sources). Nothing else about the moment is saved: no location, no device name, no camera metadata. If you refuse the permission, or your device has no camera, the App works with pictures from your gallery instead.

Pictures from your gallery (optional). If you choose "Pick from the gallery", Android's photo picker lets you select one picture; the App receives only that picture, never your whole gallery, and the App needs no storage permission. A reduced copy (about 1,400 pixels) is saved in the App's private folder as the photograph to develop. The original in your gallery is not changed.

Prints and the album (on your device only). For each photograph the App stores the developed print (a JPEG of about 1200 x 1620 pixels), the date it was developed, how many baths it has had and a time stamp. The print's number and date are printed on its frame. The forms that appear in a print are chosen by the App's own software from the photograph's shadows, edges and light together with the day it was developed, on your device; nothing is sent anywhere to decide this. You can tear up a print in the album; that removes the print and its photograph from the App. Photographs and prints are kept out of Android backups.

Sharing (only when you choose). A print leaves your device only if you tap Share: the App hands the print file to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. We do not receive it.

Settings (on your device only). Sound, vibration, language, the number of prints developed, and whether the camera permission was refused.

Motion sensor. While a print waits to be shaken, the App reads the phone's accelerometer to feel the shaking. The readings are used in memory, frame by frame, and are never stored or sent anywhere; the sensor is not read at any other time. No permission is needed for it, and without an accelerometer the print is shaken by dragging it with a finger.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never over the viewfinder and never while a print develops: a banner at the bottom of the darkroom and album screens; now and then a full-screen ad when a new photograph begins (opening the camera or the picker from the darkroom, or "another photograph" after a print; never in your first session or before the first photograph of a launch, at least two minutes apart, limited per day); and a short video only if you choose to watch one, for a stronger bath that brings one more form up in a print. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. The App asks for the camera (optional, see above), network access for advertising, and vibration (the shutter, the print sliding out, the paper flapping, the moment something comes up; switchable in Settings). It does not use your location, microphone, contacts or files, and it does not read your gallery beyond the one picture you pick.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Developing and keeping your prints and settings: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your photographs, prints and settings stay on your device until you tear up a print, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Photographs and prints are excluded from Android Auto Backup; the small settings file may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: tear up any print in the album (this also removes its photograph), or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings. Withdraw the camera permission at any time in Android's app settings; the App then works with pictures from your gallery.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

You may refuse this transfer by declining ad consent or turning off ad personalisation; personalised advertising will then not be shown. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. Automatically collected information: the App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. International transfers take place as described in section 4.

Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information; advertising data received by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries, as described in section 4.

India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to nominate and the right to grievance redressal through the contact below.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its photographs, prints and settings in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
