Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-08

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Night Watch: Overnight Recorder mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App records sound in your room while you sleep, but only if you start a night watch, and it keeps those recordings only on your device. The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. Your recordings, the reports of your nights and your settings are stored only in the App's private storage on your device; they are never uploaded, never sent to us and never included in device backups. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Settings (on your device only). Sound, vibration, the recorder's sensitivity (1 to 5), and whether the notification permission was already asked.

Microphone and recordings (on your device only). When you start a night watch, the App uses the microphone through a foreground service (shown by an ongoing notification for as long as it runs) until you end the watch, end it from the notification, or twelve hours pass. The App measures the loudness of the room every 50 milliseconds and keeps the last three seconds of sound in memory. Only when the sound rises clearly above the room's own quiet does it save a short clip (at most about 16 seconds, 16 kHz mono WAV) in its private folder; everything else it hears is discarded at once. If a night has no such moment, the App keeps its single loudest moment (about four seconds). At most 30 clips are kept per night and the last 10 nights are kept; older nights are deleted automatically when a new watch begins. Recordings may contain any sound in the room, including speech by you or by others present: start a watch only where you are allowed to record and tell anyone sharing the room. The clips are never uploaded, shared, sent to us or analysed by any service outside your device. You can play, delete one clip, delete a night or delete all recordings in the App at any time.

Night reports (on your device only). For each night the App keeps the time the watch started and ended, the loudest level of every minute (in dB), the room's quiet level, the sensitivity used, and for every kept moment its time, length, loudest level, a small outline of its shape for drawing, and a label the App gives it. The labels and the "decoded" words are produced by the App's own software from the recording's measurements and a number derived from it; they are entertainment, not speech recognition: the App does not transcribe speech and does not identify voices or people.

Motion sensor (in memory only). During a watch the App reads the accelerometer to note when the phone is moved or knocked; only the time of such an event is kept in the night's report.

Battery state. On the preparation screen the App reads whether the phone is charging and its battery level, to remind you to plug it in. This is not stored.

Text-to-speech. When you tap "Hear what it says", the App asks the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone to speak the decoded words; the sample is written to the App's cache and deleted as soon as it is played. The text-to-speech engine is provided by your device and works under its own terms.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never on the preparation screen or during a watch: a banner at the bottom of the home screen, the list of nights and the morning reports, after your first finished watch; now and then a full-screen ad when you begin a new watch (never the first watch of an app session, at least two minutes apart, limited per session and per day); and a short video only if you tap the decoder offer on a report, which says what it gives: every remaining visit of that night is decoded. Advertisements are limited to content rated PG or lower. Some advertisements may be served by Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. The SDK collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. It does not receive your recordings. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings lets you change it later. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. Microphone (the night watch, asked on the preparation screen with its reason), foreground service of type microphone and a wake lock (so the recorder keeps running with the screen off), notifications (Android 13 and later, optional: the ongoing notice of the watch and a notice when a twelve-hour watch ends), vibration (haptics; switchable in Settings), and network access (advertising). The App does not use your location, camera, contacts, call log, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Recording, keeping and playing your nights and running the App: providing the functions you request (you start every watch yourself), and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Your recordings and night reports are not transmitted to anyone. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Recordings and night reports stay on your device until you delete them in the App, until they are removed automatically (only the last 10 nights are kept), or until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. They are excluded from Android backups. Your settings stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App; the App uses Android Auto Backup for the settings only, so if backup is turned on they may be included in the backup in your own Google account. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: delete a clip, a night or all recordings in the App, or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. You can refuse or withdraw the microphone permission in Android Settings at any time; the App then cannot start a watch. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its recordings and data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, excludes the recordings from backups, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the App can play the recordings; delete them if that matters to you.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
