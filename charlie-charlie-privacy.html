Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") provides the Charlie Charlie Challenge mobile app (the "App"), an interactive horror experience that recreates the Charlie Charlie pencil ritual. This policy explains what information is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we operate no server for it. The App itself sends us nothing. What stays on your device: your settings and a few counters. What leaves your device: advertising data collected by Google AdMob, and, only if you allow the microphone, your speech, which the speech recognition service of your device turns into text. We receive nothing except what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Settings and counters (on your device only). Sound, vibration, microphone and language choices; the number of rituals held, questions answered and rituals left without a goodbye (which changes the atmosphere of the next one); and how often full-screen advertisements have been shown, to space them out. Nothing you say or ask is stored: the questions of a ritual are kept in memory only while the result screen is open.

Microphone and speech (optional). If you allow the microphone, the App uses the speech recognition service of your device (on most Android phones, Google's) to turn what you say into text, so that it can recognise the ritual phrases and your question. The App does not record audio, and it never stores or transmits audio or the recognised text; the text is used in the moment and discarded. The speech service itself may process your voice on the device or on the servers of its provider, under that provider's privacy policy (for Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time in Android Settings or turn "Listen with the microphone" off in the App's settings; the ritual then works by tapping.

Touches. Your taps are used in memory to run the ritual and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements outside the ritual only: a banner at the bottom of the home and result screens after your first ritual, a full-screen ad when you leave the result screen for the home screen (limited to a few per day and never on the first launch), and a short video only if you choose to watch one to keep asking questions. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Our other apps. The App downloads a list of our own apps and their icons from our website so that it can suggest them. This request carries no personal information beyond what any web request does (your IP address) and is served by GitHub Pages.

Permissions. Network access (advertising and the list above), vibration (effects, switchable in Settings) and the microphone (optional, described above). The App does not use your location, camera, contacts, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Running the ritual, keeping your settings and counters: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app. Speech recognition: your consent, given through the microphone permission, which you can withdraw at any time. Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free. Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection. The provider of your device's speech recognition service (on most phones Google LLC) receives your speech as described in section 2. Google processes this data under the safeguards described in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your settings and counters stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. The App uses Android Auto Backup, so if backup is on, these settings may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted. Speech text is discarded as soon as it has been used; audio is never kept. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising and speech data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

On the device: delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it; turn the microphone, sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings; withdraw the microphone permission in Android Settings.

Advertising: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland: the GDPR / UK GDPR rights in section 6 apply; you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

Republic of Korea: items processed, purposes and retention periods are those in sections 2, 3 and 5. Personal information is transferred overseas only to Google LLC (United States and other countries where it operates): the advertising data and, if you allow the microphone, your speech, as described in section 2; transmitted over the network while an advertisement is shown or while you speak; retained according to Google's policy. You may refuse these transfers by declining ad consent and by not allowing the microphone. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. The App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. Person responsible for personal information protection: the representative of Suki App Studio, sukiappstudio@gmail.com. You may also seek help from the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972). Children under 14 may not use the App without the consent of their legal guardian.

United States (including California): we do not sell personal information for money. You may opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising through the choices in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information. India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including grievance redressal through the contact below. Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App has a horror theme and is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps; the advertising SDK and the speech service use encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
