1. What the App is

The App is an entertainment experience about dowsing: you hold the phone like a pair of rods and walk slowly through a room or a yard while the rods on the screen swing, lean and cross. The rods' movement, the hidden spots and the answers in Ask mode are generated by the App's own software from a per-session seed and the motion of your phone; the App does not detect water, buried objects or anything else in the world. The App keeps your settings and the journal of your walks on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
