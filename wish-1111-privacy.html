Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the 11:11 Wish: Make a Wish mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. The wishes you write, the moments you caught and your settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. The App does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts, messages or files. If you agree to reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device; nothing is sent to us for this. The App needs a network connection only for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2, and for the list of our other apps, which is downloaded from our website. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Your wishes (on your device only). The text of each wish you write (up to 111 characters), the date, time and time zone at which it was sealed, which moment it belongs to (for example 11:11 at night), whether you marked it as come true, the look-backs you answered, and a wish you prepared that is waiting for the next moment. Wishes are never sent to us, to advertisers or to anyone else, and are never used for advertising. You can delete a single wish in the journal, or everything by clearing the App's data.

Moments and settings (on your device only). The dates and moments at which you sealed a wish (used for the streak and the count of moments caught), reminders on or off for the morning and the night, angel numbers on or off, sound, vibration, and the language you choose in the App. The number of pauses in a session (a wish opened from the journal, the sealed card closed) is counted to space out advertisements.

Reminders (optional). Reminders are off until you agree to them, either in the offer shown after your first sealed wish or in Settings. On Android 13 and later, Android then asks for the notification permission. The App schedules, on your device, a notice shortly before 11:11 in the morning and before 11:11 at night (each can be switched off), using Android's alarm service. A notice says that 11:11 is near and, if one is waiting, that your wish is waiting; it never shows the wish itself. After the phone restarts, or when the clock or time zone changes, the App sets the reminders again. Turn them off at any time in the App's Settings or in Android Settings.

Share card. If you tap "Share this moment" and then "Share", the App draws an image of the sealed moment (the time, the date and the seal; the text of your wish only if you switch on "Show my wish on the card"), saves it in its own cache folder and hands it to the app you choose through the Android share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you tap, and we never receive the image.

Our other apps. The list of our other apps in Settings is downloaded from our website https://sukiappstudio.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages) at most once a day and kept on your device. The request carries no personal information beyond what every web request carries (your IP address, which GitHub may log). Tapping an app opens Google Play.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never during the ritual: a banner at the bottom of the home screen and of the wish journal (hidden in the minute before a moment and during it); now and then a full-screen ad when you open a wish from the journal or close the sealed card (never the first time in a session, never in the minute before a moment or during it, at least two minutes apart, limited to a few per day); and a short video only if you choose to watch one to add a second wish to a seal that is still warm or to rewrite a sealed wish. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. Network access (advertising and the list of our apps), vibration (under your thumb on the seal; switchable in Settings), notifications (only if you agree to reminders) and starting after a restart (only to set the reminders again). The App does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, call log, text messages, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Keeping your wishes, the moments you caught and your settings, and sending the reminders you asked for: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your wishes, the moments you caught and your settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The App uses Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on on your device, this data may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted, and may be moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: delete a wish in the journal, or everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn reminders, sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland: the GDPR / UK GDPR rights in section 6 apply; you may complain to your local supervisory authority. Data received by Google in the United States is described in section 4.

Republic of Korea: items processed, purposes and retention periods are those in sections 2, 3 and 5. Personal information is provided or transferred overseas only to Google LLC (United States and other countries where it operates): the advertising data in section 2, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; time and method: transmitted over the network while an advertisement is shown; retention: according to Google's policy. You may refuse this transfer by declining ad consent or turning off ad personalisation; personalised advertising will then not be shown. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. Automatically collected information: the App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. Personal information is destroyed by deleting the App's data, and e-mails by deleting them from our mailbox. Person responsible for personal information protection: the representative of Suki App Studio, sukiappstudio@gmail.com. You may also seek help from the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972). Children under 14 may not use the App without the consent of their legal guardian.

United States (including California): we do not sell personal information for money. You may opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising through the choices in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. International transfers take place as described in section 4.

Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information; advertising data received by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries, as described in section 4.

India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to nominate and the right to grievance redressal through the contact below.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
