Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Spirit of the Glass mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. Your settings and your journal of past games (the questions you typed and what the glass spelled) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2, and for the "More rituals" list, which is downloaded from our website. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Settings (on your device only). Sound, vibration, language and the board you chose.

Game history (on your device only). The number of games you have finished and how the last one ended (for example whether the glass was sent back to the home circle; if a game was left unfinished, the next one starts with the glass already moving), and counters used to space out full-screen advertisements. The journal: for each game, its date and time, the board used, how many fingers were on the glass, how it ended, how often fingers were lifted from the glass, the questions you typed and what the glass spelled (at most the last 60 games). A game in progress is kept as a draft on your device so that a game cut short still appears in the journal. You can delete one game, or the whole journal with "Burn the journal", in the Journal screen. A game leaves your device only if you tap Share: the App then draws a picture of it, saves it as a temporary file in the App's cache and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share sheet; we do not receive it.

Questions and touches. The text you type and the positions of the fingers resting on the glass on the screen (one to four people may play on the same phone) are processed only on your device to run the game (to decide the answer and move the glass). The questions and answers are kept in the journal on your device as described above; finger positions are not stored. Nothing is sent anywhere. The App has no voice input and does not use the microphone or camera.

"More rituals" list. To show our other apps, the App downloads a list of them and their icons from our website sukiappstudio.github.io, at most once a day. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub, Inc. receives your IP address and the usual technical request data (such as the time of the request and the type of app or browser) as the host of the site. No information about you or your use of the App is sent with this request. The App then checks on your device which of those apps are already installed, so that it does not suggest them; this result never leaves your device.

Advertising data (collected by Google and its partners). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never during a game: a banner at the bottom of the home and result screens, after your first finished game; now and then a full-screen ad when you begin a new game (never before the first one of a session, at most every second start, at least two minutes apart, and limited to a few per day); and a short video only if you choose to watch one to light a new candle for three more questions. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Google may request advertisements from partner networks through its mediation; one of them is Unity Technologies (Unity Ads), which may then receive advertising data as described in its privacy policy at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. The App asks for network access (to show advertising and the "More rituals" list) and vibration (used for the glass's movements and other effects, and switchable in Settings), and no other permission. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Keeping your journal and settings, running the game and showing the "More rituals" list: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising; mediation partners such as Unity Technologies (United States and other countries) may receive advertising data through Google's mediation for the same purposes. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. GitHub, Inc. (United States), which hosts our website, receives the request data described in section 2 when the App downloads the "More rituals" list. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your journal and settings stay on your device until you delete them in the App, clear the App's data or uninstall the App; the journal keeps at most the last 60 games, and older ones are removed automatically. The picture made for sharing is a temporary file in the App's cache, which Android may clear at any time. The App uses Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on on your device, this data may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted, and may be moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google, its advertising partners and GitHub retain the data they receive according to their own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: delete one game or the whole journal ("Burn the journal") in the Journal screen, or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

Russia: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, and the advertising SDK and the download of the "More rituals" list use encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
