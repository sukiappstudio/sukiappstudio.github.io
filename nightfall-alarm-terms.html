1. What the App is

The App is an alarm clock with a horror theme. You set alarms; when one rings, the App plays one of its own synthesised hauntings (knocking, a music box, footsteps, a whispering child, a radio voice) or, in safe mode, a gentle chime, and shows a dark room in which a figure drawn for the App comes one step closer with every snooze. You stop the alarm by drawing a circle on the screen or by holding a key. The App keeps a record of nights on your device and can make a morning card for you to share. The hauntings and the figure are fiction created for the App; they portray no real person, and nothing in the App communicates with anyone. The App runs on your device and has no accounts and no features for communicating with other users.
