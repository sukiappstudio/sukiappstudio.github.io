1. What the App is

The App is a single-player, interactive retelling of the Satoru-kun urban legend: a public telephone, a ten-yen coin, your own number, and his calls over the following day, ending with one question. Everything is produced by the App's own software on your device. The "calls" are notifications from the App, not telephone calls, and no call is ever placed from or to your number. The App keeps your settings, the night in progress and the record of past nights on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.

5. Play safely

The App is a work of fiction, designed as an atmospheric experience with dim imagery, sound, vibration and calls that may arrive at night, including while you sleep, for up to twenty-four hours after you hang up. Start a night only when that suits you; you can end it at any time from the App, and your phone's own settings can silence notifications. If you find the experience distressing, stop using it; sound and vibration can be turned off in Settings. Do not use the App while driving, walking in traffic or doing anything that needs your attention, and do not act out the legend at a real public telephone at night or in any way that puts you at risk. His "answers" are generated at random by the App: do not base decisions about your health, safety, relationships, money or any other matter on them. Anyone who is worried or frightened should talk to someone they trust.
