1. What the App is

The App is a horror voice changer experience: you hold a candle, speak, let go, and a dark figure speaks your words back in one of fourteen voices made by the App's own sound processing from your recording (changes of pitch and timing, reversal, modulation, distortion and synthesised reverberation). You can keep, replay and share the result as a video or sound file. In the question mode the figure replies with your own recording turned around and one word chosen by the App's software from fixed lists, by your recording and the day. The voices are sound effects; they are the App's own creations, portray no real or historical person and reproduce nobody's voice. Nothing in the App communicates with any person or any other party, and nothing it shows or plays is a message from anyone. The App runs entirely on your device, has no accounts and no features for communicating with other users.
