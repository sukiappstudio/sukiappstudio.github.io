1. What the App is

The App is a single-player, interactive recreation of the Brazilian school legend "Loira do Banheiro" (the blonde woman of the school bathroom): the corridor, the girls' bathroom, flushing the last toilet three times, kicking the stall door three times, saying her name three times to the mirror, her answers by the tap under the mirror (running once for yes, twice for no), the goodbye, and leaving without looking in the mirror. It runs entirely on your device. Everything that happens in it, including the answers and the figure, is produced by the App's own software; the App does not contact anyone or anything. The App keeps your settings and the outcome of your rituals on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
