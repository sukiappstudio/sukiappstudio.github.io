Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Dowsing Rods: Spirit Finder mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. Your settings and the journal of your walks are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. While a walk is on screen the App reads the phone's motion sensors (the gyroscope-based rotation sensor and the accelerometer) only in memory; it does not use your location, the camera or the microphone, and no sensor data is ever stored as such or sent anywhere. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Settings (on your device only). Audio, haptics, the sensitivity of the rods (1 to 5) and the scale you chose last (a room or a yard).

The journal (on your device only). For each finished walk, up to 100 walks, the App keeps: the date and time it started, its duration, whether it was a room or a yard, the number of steps and the distance in metres, how many hidden spots there were and which of them you found (their kind: presence, cold spot, buried or water; their position in metres relative to where the walk started; and when they were found), how many questions you asked and how many answers were yes, no or unclear, the path you walked as up to 240 points in metres relative to the start, whether the rods were sensitized, and the place name you may type. The text of the questions you type is not stored. You can delete the whole journal with "Clear journal" in the App.

Other values (on your device only). To space out advertisements the App keeps the time of the last full-screen ad, how many were shown today and the number of finished walks; after you watch the "Sensitize the rods" video it also keeps the time at which the ten minutes end.

Sensors (in memory only). While a walk is on screen the App reads the gyroscope through the game rotation vector (or the rotation vector on phones without it): the direction the phone faces and how it turns, so that the rods follow your turning. It also reads the accelerometer: steps are counted from the phone's up-and-down bounce, and the movement and tilt of your hands push the rods. The App does not use the step-counter sensor or the activity recognition permission. Your position is estimated only as steps and turns relative to where the walk started, in metres, never as a place on Earth; the App does not use GPS or any other kind of location. The readings are processed in memory on your device and only the journal figures above are kept. Nothing from the sensors identifies you.

Your questions (in memory only). In Ask mode you may type a question. It stays in memory for that walk only and is used on your device to decide the rods' answer; it is never stored or sent.

Share card. If you tap "Share card", the App draws an image of one journal entry (the map of the walk and its figures), saves it in its own cache folder and hands it to the app you choose through the Android share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you tap, and we never receive the image.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways. No advertisement is shown while you settle the rods or during a walk, apart from the offers you tap yourself. A banner at the bottom of the home screen, the journal and a journal entry, after your first finished walk; now and then a full-screen ad when you start a new walk (never before the first walk of an app session, at least two minutes apart, limited per session and per day); and a short video only if you tap an offer that explains what it gives: "Sensitize the rods" (for ten minutes the rods react from farther away and cross a little sooner) or "Reveal one more spot" (one more hidden spot is placed ahead of you). Advertisements are limited to content rated PG (parental guidance) or lower. Some advertisements may be served by Unity Ads through AdMob mediation. The SDK collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings lets you change it later. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. Internet and network state access (advertising) and vibration (haptics when the rods cross; switchable in Settings). That is all: the App does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, call log, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Keeping your settings and journal and running the App: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your settings, your journal and the other values in section 2 stay on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App; the journal can also be deleted at any time with "Clear journal" in the App. The App uses Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on on your device, this data may be included in the backup in your own Google account until that backup is deleted, and may be moved to a new phone when you transfer your data. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: delete the journal with "Clear journal" in the App, or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
