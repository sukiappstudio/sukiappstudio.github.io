The App gives palm readings and face readings for entertainment. It looks at a photo of your palm or your face, measures visible features on your device (for example the lines of the palm, the hand shape and the proportions of the face) and shows how traditional palmistry and face reading (Western, Chinese, Korean and Japanese traditions) interpret those features. It can also compare two saved readings ("compatibility").
