1. What the App is

The App is a set of outdoor tools for hikers: a compass, your position in several coordinate formats (including Korea's national point number), an altimeter and air-pressure trend, sunrise, sunset and moon times, saved places with directions back to them, a hike timer with a turnaround reminder, a flashlight with SOS signal and red light, a whistle and siren, a slope meter, a gear checklist, an SOS screen with emergency numbers and position sharing, and a short safety and first-aid guide.

2. Not a substitute for preparation or rescue services

The App relies on a phone's GPS, sensors and battery. Positions, altitudes, directions, distances, times and slope angles are approximate and can be wrong or unavailable, for example under tree cover, in valleys, near metal or magnets, in cold weather, or when the battery is empty. The emergency numbers shown are a convenience and may be out of date or not the right service where you are.

Do not rely on the App as your only means of navigation, timekeeping or calling for help. Carry a map, a real compass, a headlamp and enough clothing, food and water; check the weather and the route; tell someone your plan; and follow the instructions of park authorities and rescue services. The safety guide is general information, not medical advice and not a replacement for first-aid training or professional care. In an emergency, call the local emergency number first.

The flashlight's strobe can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy; do not point it at people's eyes. The whistle and siren are loud; do not hold the phone to your ear while they play.
