1. What the App is

The App is a single-player interactive story that retells the Kisaragi Station internet legend: the night of 8 January 2004 as it unfolded in an anonymous bulletin-board thread, seen from the phone of the woman on the train. The replies of the thread, the sounds and the scenes are produced by the App's own software, offline. You choose what she posts and what she does; different choices lead to different endings. The App keeps your settings and your progress on your device. It has no accounts, no real chat and no multiplayer features; the "board" inside the App is not connected to any real website and nobody else can read or answer what is posted in it.
