1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror fiction told through telephone calls. You choose one of its original characters and a time; at that time the App lets you know with an ordinary notification, and the call plays inside the App: the character speaks, you choose your answers, and the call reaches one of several endings. The calls are fiction. They are not real phone calls: the App does not use the telephone network, does not call or text anyone, and does not show the system's call screen. The characters, their stories and their voices (made by your phone's text-to-speech engine) are created by the App. The App keeps your settings, the optional name the callers use and your call log on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.

The App is designed to be frightening: dark images, unsettling voices and stories, a bell that rings at night, and vibration. It is meant for adults and teenagers who enjoy horror.
