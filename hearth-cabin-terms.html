1. What the App is

The App is an atmospheric sound experience: a cabin in the snow with a fireplace you tend yourself. You strike a match, put logs from a basket on the fire and stir it with a poker, and the App synthesizes the sound of the fire, the wind and the cabin live on your device. Each night has its own weather, sometimes a guest and a wood of the night; there are three cabins, a journal of woods and nights, a sound mixer, a sleep timer and an optional evening notice. Everything the App does is produced by its own software on your device. The App keeps your settings, your fire and your journal on your device. It has no accounts and no features for communicating with other users.
