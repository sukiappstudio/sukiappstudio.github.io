1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror ritual based on a Japanese urban legend. You play a bath rite inside the App (turning off a light, filling a bath, calling a name, counting with your eyes shut) and afterwards a figure follows you for the time you choose; short checks arrive as ordinary notifications, and at sundown you tell her to stop. The bathroom, the figure, her voice and everything that happens are fiction created by the App; you do not need, and must not use, a real bath to play. The App keeps your settings and your past rites on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.

If you find the App distressing, if it disturbs your sleep or you are sensitive to sudden sounds, do not use it, choose a short rite, or turn sound and vibration off in Settings.
