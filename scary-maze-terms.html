1. What the App is

The App is a two-person maze experience: one person sets up a round (a figure, a sound, a moment), then hands the phone to another person, who guides a dot through three narrowing corridors with one finger. At the moment set, the screen cuts to black and a figure appears with a loud sound and strong vibration. The figures are our own photographic composites and drawings; the sounds are synthesised in the App. With the agreement of the person holding the phone, given on their own screen before the round, the App keeps six frames from the front camera and prints them on a reaction card. It runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
