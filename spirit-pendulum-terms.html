The App is an atmospheric divination experience for entertainment. It shows a pendulum hanging over a divination board. The pendulum is moved by a physical model driven by your phone's real motion sensors and, when you ask a question, by the App's answer layer: the App's software chooses an answer from your question and a seed for each sitting and guides the swing towards it on the board.
