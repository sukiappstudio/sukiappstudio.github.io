1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror ritual based on an anonymous Japanese internet legend. You prepare a room with salt and a paper charm and start a night that runs in real time until the dawn you choose; during the night a figure comes to the window and imitates the voices of people you know, and you must not answer. Visits, dawn and the end of a night arrive as ordinary notifications when the App is closed. The story, the voices and everything that happens are fiction created by the App; the voices are made by your phone's text-to-speech engine. The App keeps your settings, the optional name the voices use and your past nights on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
