1. What the App is

The App is a single-player interactive horror story: you find a fictional woman’s phone and read its messages, photographs, voice memos, notes and map, reply to the characters by choosing among answers, and reach one of several endings. The story is told in chapters; between chapters the story’s phone “charges” until a set time. The phone, the people, the places, the photographs, the sounds and the texts are invented and made for the App; they are not about any real person, place or event. The story’s phone exists only inside the App: it does not send real messages, make real calls or show notifications on your device. The App has no accounts, no chat with real people and no multiplayer features.
