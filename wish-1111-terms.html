1. What the App is

The App is a wish ritual built around the moments when the clock shows 11:11, in the morning and at night (and, if you choose, 12:12 and 22:22). It shows the time and a countdown to the next moment, guides a short breath, lets you write one wish and seal it by holding your thumb on a wax seal as the clock turns, and keeps your sealed wishes in a journal on your device. It can remind you shortly before each 11:11. It has no accounts and no social features. The App is a personal ritual for reflection and intention; it does not promise or predict any outcome, and whether a wish comes true does not depend on the App.
