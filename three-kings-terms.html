1. What the App is

The App is an interactive horror experience built around the "Three Kings", a ritual that spread on the internet. It guides you through the steps of the ritual on your phone's real clock: the room the evening before, the alarm at 3:30, the throne at 3:33, the call at 4:34. On the screen it shows the room, the two mirrors and a candle that reacts to your breath, your phone's movement and the hour, and it plays sounds, vibrations and visual effects that tell a story of fear. The App does not summon anything, cannot predict or influence events, and makes no claim about the supernatural. The answers the mirrors give are produced by the App's own software. It keeps your names, journal and settings on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.

Do not use the App if you are prone to anxiety attacks, have a heart condition that frightening content could affect, or are sensitive to flashing or sudden images and sounds.

3. Your safety comes first

The ritual involves a real candle, a fan, a bucket of water, large mirrors propped on chairs and a dark room in the middle of the night. You decide whether to use any real object at all; the App works entirely with the candle and the room on the screen. If you do use real objects, you do so at your own risk and must act responsibly: keep a lit candle in a holder away from anything flammable and away from the fan, never leave it unattended and never go to sleep with it burning; prop mirrors so they cannot fall; keep water away from electrical appliances; do not walk through a dark house in a way that could make you fall; and never throw water at or startle a person who may have a heart condition or is otherwise vulnerable. Do not perform the steps while driving, in traffic or anywhere that needs your attention. Waking at 3:30 costs sleep: do not do this on a night before you must drive or work. Stop at once if you feel unwell. Nothing in the App requires you to put yourself or others in any danger, and we are not responsible for injury or damage that results from how you choose to act around real candles, fans, water, mirrors or darkness.

4. The real clock and leaving the App

The night runs on your phone's clock from the evening you arm it until 4:34 whether or not the App is open. The App schedules an alarm for 3:30 through Android; whether it can wake you depends on your phone's settings (exact alarms, notifications, battery optimisation, sound) and we make no promise that it will. Keeping the screen on for an hour uses battery; the App does not lock the device and you can leave it at any time. We make no promise that a night in progress survives a restart, a battery that runs out, a change of the system time or an update.
