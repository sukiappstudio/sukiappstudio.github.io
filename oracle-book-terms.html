1. What the App is

The App is a single-player book oracle experience: you hold a question in mind or type it, rest your hand on the cover of an old book, riffle its pages and stop them anywhere, and read the line printed on the page where the book opens, with a short note. The book, its pages and the room are drawn by the App. Where the book opens is chosen by the App's own software from your words, the day and the leaf where you stopped the pages, and the lines and notes are fixed texts written for the App. The App runs entirely on your device and keeps your settings and your journal there. It has no accounts, no chat and no multiplayer features.
