Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-25

Suki App Studio ("we", "us") is the controller of personal information processed through the Lucky Day Almanac mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what is processed, by whom and why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

The App has no user accounts and we do not operate any server for it. The calendar is computed on your device; the days you mark and your settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone. The App works without a network connection except for advertising, which is provided by Google AdMob and collects the data described in section 2, and for the list of our other apps, which is fetched from our website without any identifier. We receive nothing from the App itself, only what you send us by e-mail.

2. Information processed

Settings (on your device only). The almanac tradition, the first day of the week, the purpose last chosen, the notice hour, sound, vibration and language.

Marked days (on your device only). The dates you mark and, if you chose one, the purpose for each. They are kept in the App's preferences, may be included in Android's backup of the App's settings, and can be removed one by one in the App. A marked day never leaves your device.

Year sheets (on your device only). If you draw a year sheet, the App renders an image of the year's favourable days for a purpose and saves it in its private storage, excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. It leaves your device only if you tap Share: the App then hands the image to the app you pick in Android's share sheet; we do not receive it.

Notices (optional). If you mark a day, the App asks for permission to show notifications (on Android 13 and newer) and sets one local notice for the evening before that day, using an inexact alarm. The notice is created on your device and sends nothing anywhere. The App also receives the system's "boot completed" signal only to set the next notice again after a restart. You can unmark a day, or deny notifications in Android's settings, at any time.

Advertising data (collected by Google). The Google Mobile Ads SDK shows advertisements in three ways, never on a day's page or in the guide: a banner at the bottom of the month and finder screens; now and then a full-screen ad when you turn to another month (never in your first session after installing the App or on the first turn of a launch, at least two minutes apart, limited per day); and a short video only if you choose to watch one, to draw the year sheet. It collects, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, the IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, diagnostic information, and how you interact with advertisements. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any ad is requested. Google LLC and its partners process this data as described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App has no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

Permissions. Besides network access for advertising and our app list, the App asks only for vibration (turning the leaf, a seal landing; switchable in Settings), notifications (only if you mark a day) and the boot signal described above. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

E-mail. If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and the content of your message and use them only to answer you.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Keeping your settings and marked days and computing the calendar: providing the functions you request, and our legitimate interest in offering a working app. A notice is set only for a day you mark yourself, and only if you allow notifications.

Advertising: your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, collected through Google's consent message), otherwise our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in responding to you and dealing with your request.

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not sell personal information. We receive none of the data described in section 2 except what you send us by e-mail. The days you mark are not transferred to anyone. Google Play also gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports that do not identify you.

Google LLC, in the United States and other countries where Google and its advertising partners operate, receives the advertising data described in section 2 directly from your device, over an encrypted connection, to show and measure advertising. Google processes it under the safeguards it describes in its policies, including standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Our e-mail is hosted by Google (Gmail). Under some laws, showing personalised advertising is treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out as described in section 6.

5. Retention

Your marked days, year sheets and settings stay on your device until you remove them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Year sheets are excluded from Android Auto Backup; your settings and marked days may be included in Android Auto Backup in your own Google account if backup is turned on on your device, until that backup is deleted. We keep e-mail only as long as needed to deal with your message, unless the law requires longer. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

Your data on the device: unmark a day on its page or in the marked-days list, or delete everything by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. Turn sound and vibration on or off in the App's settings; allow or deny notifications in Android's settings.

Advertising choices: open "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings (shown where the law requires it) to change or withdraw your consent; reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we hold no personal information about you on any server, most requests are fulfilled on your own device; for data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can always contact us at the address below and we will answer within the period required by law.

7. Regional information

European Economic Area (including Greece and Cyprus), United Kingdom, Switzerland: the GDPR / UK GDPR rights in section 6 apply; you may complain to your local supervisory authority (in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority). Data received by Google in the United States is described in section 4.

Türkiye: we process personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). You have the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK. The advertising data in section 2 is transferred abroad to Google LLC as described in section 4, on the basis of your explicit consent where the law requires it. You may also apply to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Middle East and North Africa: if you live in a country with a personal data protection law (for example the United Arab Emirates, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Bahrain, Qatar or Jordan), you have the rights that law gives you, including access, correction, deletion and objection, and the transfers in section 4 take place on the basis of your consent or the safeguards that law allows.

Republic of Korea: items processed, purposes and retention periods are those in sections 2, 3 and 5. Personal information is provided or transferred overseas only to Google LLC (United States and other countries where it operates): the advertising data in section 2, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; time and method: transmitted over the network while an advertisement is shown; retention: according to Google's policy. You may refuse this transfer by declining ad consent or turning off ad personalisation; personalised advertising will then not be shown. Our e-mail service (Gmail) is entrusted to Google LLC. Automatically collected information: the App uses no cookies; the advertising SDK uses the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete as described in section 6. Personal information is destroyed by deleting the App's data, and e-mails by deleting them from our mailbox. Person responsible for personal information protection: the representative of Suki App Studio, sukiappstudio@gmail.com. You may also seek help from the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (https://privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (https://www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972). Children under 14 may not use the App without the consent of their legal guardian.

United States (including California): we do not sell personal information for money. You may opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising through the choices in section 6. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Brazil: you have the rights listed in Article 18 of the LGPD and may contact the ANPD. International transfers take place as described in section 4.

Japan: we handle personal information in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information; advertising data received by Google may be processed in the United States and other countries, as described in section 4.

India: you have the rights provided by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to nominate and the right to grievance redressal through the contact below.

Russia, Vietnam: you have the rights provided by your national personal data law, including to withdraw consent and to request deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Anyone below the age at which they can consent to the processing of their personal data where they live (for example 14 in the Republic of Korea or up to 16 in some European countries) may use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly process children's personal information. If you believe a child has used the App without such consent, contact us.

9. Security

The App stores its data in the private storage that Android protects from other apps, keeps the year sheets out of cloud backup, and the advertising SDK uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes

We will post any update to this policy in the App and on its store page, with a new effective date, before the change takes effect.
