1. What the App is

The App is a storytelling experience in the style of hyakumonogatari, the old Japanese gathering of a hundred ghost stories, and of English Christmas Eve ghost stories. It shows a room of candles, presents ghost stories to read or hear, and lets you put out a candle after each one; it also keeps the candles for a gathering in which you and your friends tell your own stories. The stories, the room, the sounds and the other texts were written or made for the App. They are fiction: any resemblance to actual persons, places or events is coincidental. The App does not contact anyone, living or dead. The App runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no chat and no online features.
